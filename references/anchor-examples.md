# Calibration anchors (cold start)

> **What this is:** a fixed set of graded outfits that shows what each band of the /5 scale looks like, for a client whose own outfit history is still thin. The loader injects it until he has 10+ graded outfits of his own **and** his own anti-examples, then it retires automatically.
>
> **Never propose these outfits and never present them as his history.** They belong to a fictional reference client. **Transfer the failure type and its cost, not the colours:** a "single off-palette accessory near the face" costs a few tenths for every client, whatever his season. His own grades always outrank these anchors when the two disagree.

**Reference client (fictional):** Theo, 29, 183cm, slim/rectangle frame (narrow shoulders, straight torso). True Summer colouring (cool, soft, medium-low contrast): his neutral register is navy, slate, soft charcoal, cool gray and soft white; his earth register is stone, oatmeal, cool taupe and mushroom; his heroes are soft teal, raspberry and periwinkle. Signature silhouette: fitted-to-regular top with some structure + straight or relaxed bottom; never oversized top on an oversized bottom (it swamps his frame). Aesthetic: smart-casual minimal. Temperate city.

## What the anchors teach, in order of cost

1. **Proportion violations are the most expensive** (R14 at 2.6, R15 at 2.3, R13 at 2.9). No palette rescues them, and they compound with other violations.
2. **Stacked violations compound** (R13): two moderate failures land below 3.0.
3. **Stacked loudness costs real points but isn't fatal on its own** (R9 at 3.6) when the palette and silhouette are still coherent.
4. **Formality incoherence and un-bridged register gaps** land in the low-to-mid 3s (R12 at 3.2, R11 at 3.4).
5. **Weather misses** cost about a point and a half, even when everything else is right (R10).
6. **A single off-palette item near the face costs a few tenths**, not a full point, when it's small and everything else is clean (R7 at 3.7).
7. **Texture is the difference between washed out and the top of the scale** inside one register (R8 at 3.6 vs R1 at 4.9).
8. **Doing nothing wrong is worth about 3.9** (R6). The gap between 3.9 and 4.5 is entirely elevating elements; 4.9 needs everything plus a reason the outfit is *the* call for the occasion.
