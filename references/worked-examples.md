# Worked examples (gold standard output)

Load when running a mode and you want the target shape of the answer. These are **format and quality references**, not templates to copy: never reuse these specific outfits as if they were freshly reasoned, and never treat this client's closet as the user's.

All three are for the fictional reference client from the calibration anchors: **Theo**, 29, 183cm, slim/rectangle frame, True Summer (cool, soft, medium-low contrast), smart-casual minimal, temperate city. Note what they have in common: honest grade spread, a named cap on every option, argument rather than recap, and zero item ids in the client-facing text.

---

## Mode A: outfit-build

**Prompt:** "Drinks with friends Friday, somewhere decent but not fancy."

**Read:** formality ~3, late May, 22C at 21:00, light layers optional. Open request, so 3 wide lanes: earth light-on-light, neutral tonal, hero on a neutral base; plus the wildcard. No high-contrast block lane: his contrast is medium-low.

**One stylist's return** (what the judge sees, never shown to him):

```
CANDIDATE 1, Stone and Oat
Lane: earth, light-on-light
- Top: 12 open-knit short-sleeve polo, oatmeal
- Bottom: 18 relaxed pleated trousers, stone
- Shoes: 27 suede loafers, taupe
- Accessories: 31 steel watch; no-show socks; no belt
Argument: Light-on-light in a committed earth register ... (2-4 sentences)
What caps it: loafers sit a half-step dressy for a casual bar.
```

**What he sees:**

12 candidates from 4 stylists, 1 failed the hard rules, best 4 below.

---

**Option A, Stone and Oat** 4.7/5

- Top: oatmeal open-knit short-sleeve polo
- Bottom: stone relaxed pleated trousers
- Shoes: taupe suede loafers
- Accessories: steel watch, no-show socks, no belt

Light-on-light in a fully committed earth register, the softest value story and the right one for medium-low contrast colouring. Every piece sits within half a value step, so texture does all the work: open knit against crisp pleated twill against napped suede is what stops a pale column reading flat. The polo's collar and knit weight add the structure his narrow shoulders want, while the relaxed pleat gives the leg presence. It is the only option with no dark note at all, so it reads lightest and most relaxed of the four.

**What caps it:** loafers sit a half-step dressy for a casual bar; a clean suede sneaker would land the formality exactly.

---

**Option B, Slate Tonal** 4.5/5

- Layer: slate cotton overshirt, open
- Top: soft-white knit tee
- Bottom: charcoal relaxed trousers
- Shoes: white leather sneakers
- Accessories: silver watch

Tonal cheat code in the neutral register, with the overshirt doing two jobs: it builds the shoulder line his slim frame lacks and adds a texture layer over the flat knit. Charcoal instead of black keeps the contrast inside what his soft colouring carries. Where A is one continuous light field, this one steps down in value from top to bottom.

**What caps it:** every piece is safe; nothing in it would surprise his friends.

---

**Option C, Teal Note** 4.4/5

- Top: soft teal fine-gauge knit polo
- Bottom: light gray straight trousers
- Shoes: white leather sneakers
- Accessories: silver watch

Hero near the face on a quiet neutral base, the single statement, everything else supporting. Soft teal is one of his clearest heroes because it is cool and muted at once, exactly his colouring. The fine gauge and collar keep formality at 3.

**What caps it:** straight gray trousers under a fitted polo is correct but flat proportion; a relaxed leg would give it shape.

---

**Option D, Navy Column** 4.2/5

- Top: navy merino crew
- Bottom: navy chinos
- Shoes: white leather sneakers

Tonal navy, the most reliable formula he has, broken once at the feet.

**What caps it:** merino on a 22C evening runs warm, and with no texture contrast or hero the outfit has nothing elevating it. This is the "default combo" pattern the scale punishes.

---

**Verdict: Option A.** It beats B on distinctiveness (texture story versus safe tonal), beats C on proportion (relaxed pleat versus flat straight leg), and beats D on weather and elevation. The full committed earth palette with three textures is the highest-confidence call for his colouring, not just the highest-scoring one.

**What this exercised:**
- **Committed register:** all four stay inside one palette; none of them hedge across two.
- **Texture over logo:** A and B outscore D purely on fabric interest at identical formality.
- **Signature silhouette:** the structure-up-top rule for a slim frame decides the layer in B and the collar in A and C.

## Mode B: purchase-judge

**Prompt:** "Found black leather loafers on sale, 90 euros. Worth it?"

**Verdict: SKIP.** Two gates fail.

1. **Duplicate slot, gate 2.** He already owns taupe suede loafers and dark brown Chelseas: two formality-4 leather shoes covering both registers. A third is not new capability.
2. **Palette, gate 4.** Black leather at formality 4 is a hard colour for True Summer colouring: it reads severe against his soft contrast, and none of his trousers are dark enough to carry it without becoming a value-orphan under a light bottom.

If the 90 euro price is the appeal rather than the loafers themselves, that is the sale tell: a discount on something he doesn't need is still spending.

**Buy instead, from his queue:**
1. **Unstructured navy blazer.** His formality-5 column is empty, which is why every wedding strains, and the blazer also builds the shoulder line his frame wants. Invest.
2. **Dark-wash straight jeans.** Every jean he owns is mid or light; dark wash is the dressiest denim and pairs with both of his leather shoes. Invest in the fit.

## Mode C: outfit-grade

**Prompt:** "Wore this today: mustard oversized hoodie, skinny ripped jeans, chunky boots. Thoughts?"

**Grade: 2.3/5.** Three hard constraints, so it sits well below the propose floor.

**What carries it:** honestly, very little; the boots themselves are a good pair.

**What breaks it:**
1. **Off-palette at the face.** Mustard is warm and saturated; on his cool, soft colouring it drains the face more than any other single choice in the outfit.
2. **Chunky boots on a skinny jean.** Chunky shoes need volume above them; on a skinny leg the boot reads bolted on.
3. **The silhouette:** wide at the top, choked in the middle, heavy at the bottom, which is the opposite of the structured-top, straight-leg shape his slim frame needs.

**Minimal swap:** keep the boots, swap the hoodie for his slate overshirt over the soft-white tee, and the skinny jeans for his straight dark chinos. Two swaps fix all three violations: cool colour at the face, a straight leg that carries the boots, and structure up top.

**Re-grade: 4.1/5**, worth almost two points.

Still capped there because chunky boots with a smart overshirt is a formality stretch; his suede Chelseas would push it to about 4.4.

**What this exercised:**
- **Signature silhouette:** two of the three failures trace back to proportion.
- **Hero near the face** (inverted): whatever sits at the face carries the most weight, which is why the wrong colour there costs the most.
