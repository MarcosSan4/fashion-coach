## Lessons
- #3: he graded a fully dressy formality-4 look 0.6 below the rules, on feel, not on a visible flaw: the pleats read as someone else's clothes. Ease him into formality 4 with one casual anchor (a knit, suede, or the chinos) until a trouser he likes is bought.
