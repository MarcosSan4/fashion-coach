# Shopping: gaps, queue, sourcing

> **SAMPLE DATA:** Alex is a fictional demo client. Every gap, price and queue entry below is made up to show how the coach plans. Delete this `wardrobe/` folder (or run from another folder) to start your own.

Seeded from the capsule blueprint after closet intake (2026-08-18). Every buy still passes the coach's six purchase gates.

## Known gaps
- **Unstructured blazer (soft navy or warm charcoal):** no blazer at all, and formality 4-5 is his thinnest band. Client presentations happen twice a month and weddings once or twice a year. It also gives a slim frame the shoulder structure the profile asks for, and pairs with the chinos, cords, oxford, merino crew and loafers he already owns.
- **A flat-front tailored trouser he will actually wear (warm charcoal or soft navy):** the only formality-4 trouser (pleated wool) is parked (see issues). Without it every client-day build drops to chinos.
- **Dark-wash jeans:** the one pair is mid-wash and skews casual; a dark indigo straight leg is the denim that works with the merino crew and loafers.
- **Turtleneck in his palette (chocolate brown or warm charcoal):** the black one is off-palette near the face; a warm one is the winter layer under the overcoat and the future blazer.
- ~~**Plain tees in his neutrals:**~~ closed 2026-08-18, heavyweight crew tee owned in ivory, warm gray and soft navy. A further tee is a duplicate slot, replace-on-wear only.

## Purchase priority order (highest leverage first)
1. Unstructured blazer, soft navy: the biggest formality unlock, makes at least 5 outfits with what he owns. *Invest.*
2. Flat-front wool trousers, warm charcoal, straight leg: unlocks formality 4 with the oxford, merino crew, loafers and the future blazer. *Invest (fit).*
3. Dark-wash straight jeans: dressier denim for Fridays and dinners. *Mid.*
4. Turtleneck, chocolate brown merino: replaces the black one for winter layering. *Mid.*
5. Terracotta or dusty-rose knit: a second hero for the summer and shoulder seasons, only after 1 to 4. *Save.*

## Sourcing
- Save: Uniqlo, Arket. Mid: COS, Massimo Dutti. Invest: Our Legacy, Drake's knitwear, a made-to-measure blazer when one is on offer.
- Tailor: yes, a few minutes from the studio; hems and waist suppression are cheap there, so buy to the shoulders and tailor the rest.

## Personal notes on the gates
- **Gate 2:** tees, knit polos and casual sneakers are full slots.
- **Gate 3 (season):** linen and anything summer-only is dead from October to May in Lyon; weigh shoulder-season and winter pieces higher.
- **Gate 4:** no black above the waist, nothing optic white or saturated near the face, no slim-tapered legs.
