# Pleated wool trousers barely worn

> **SAMPLE DATA:** Alex is a fictional demo client. This issue is made up to show how parked issues work. Delete this `wardrobe/` folder (or run from another folder) to start your own.

**Problem:** the warm-gray pleated wool trousers (his only formality-4 trouser) have been worn twice since he bought them. His words, 2026-09-12: "Looked fine in the mirror but I felt like I was wearing my dad's clothes. The pleats are not me."

**Read:** not a palette or fit fault (the colour sits in his neutral register, the leg is relaxed straight, the rise suits him). It is a taste mismatch: a high-rise pleated trouser reads older and dressier than his Scandi-minimal grammar, and with no casual anchor the whole outfit turned costume (outfit #3, graded 3.8).

**Status:** parked by his choice, not a to-do. The trousers carry `flag=low-wear-parked` in the closet, so the coach never offers them as a default partner. The flat-front trouser in the shopping queue (#2) replaces their job.

**Revisit if:** he asks to try them again with a casual anchor (lambswool knit and chukkas), or wants to sell or donate them once the flat-front pair is bought.
